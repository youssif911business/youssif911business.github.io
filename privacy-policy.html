Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

🔒 Image Converter does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your files never leave your device.

Who We Are

Image Converter is an image converter app developed by Formatly Studio. This privacy policy explains how our app handles your data.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data of any kind. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect or transmit your photos or files
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not collect device identifiers beyond what AdMob requires
  • We do not use analytics tools
  • We do not store any data on our servers because we have no servers

How Image Conversion Works

All image conversion happens entirely on your device. When you convert a file, it is processed locally using your phone's own processing power. No file is ever uploaded to any server. No internet connection is required for conversion.

Advertising

The free version of Formatly displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect anonymized data about ad interactions as described in Google's privacy policy. This data is collected by Google, not by Formatly Studio. Pro subscribers see no ads.

To learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Formatly Studio does not store or process any payment information. Purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat, which processes transaction receipts to verify subscription status.

Permissions

Formatly requests the following permissions:

  • Read media images — to let you select images for conversion
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and below) — to save converted files to your gallery

These permissions are used only for their stated purpose and are never used to collect data.

Children's Privacy

Formatly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at: support@formatly.app
