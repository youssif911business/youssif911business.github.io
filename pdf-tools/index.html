Privacy

Your PDFs contain
real documents.

Contracts, ID scans, bank statements, medical records. These aren't files to send to a stranger's server. PDF Tools processes everything on your device and nowhere else.

Step 01
You pick a file

From your phone's storage. It stays there. Nothing is copied or cached anywhere else.

Step 02
Processing happens on-device

The operation runs inside the app using local libraries. No network request is made at any point.

Step 03
Result saved to your phone

The output goes directly to your Downloads folder. That's the entire journey.
