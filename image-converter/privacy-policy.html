Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

🔒 Image Converter does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your files never leave your device.

Who We Are

Image Converter is developed by Formatly. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data of any kind. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect or transmit your photos or files
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use analytics tools
  • We do not store any data on servers because we have no servers

How Conversion Works

All image conversion happens entirely on your device. Files are processed locally using your phone's own processing power. No file is ever uploaded anywhere. No internet connection is required for conversion.

Advertising

The free version displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect anonymized data about ad interactions as described in Google's privacy policy. This data is collected by Google, not by Formatly. Pro subscribers see no ads.

In-App Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Formatly does not store or process any payment information. Purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat.

Permissions

  • Read media images — to let you select images for conversion
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and below) — to save converted files
